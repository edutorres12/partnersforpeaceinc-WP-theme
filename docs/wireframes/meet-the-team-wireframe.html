1. HeaderSame as all pages. The nav item for this page is the current one.
2. HeroPhoto band with a dark wash, like the Services hero. Carries the page's ONE H1 — every name below is an H2, so the outline reads as one page about a team rather than six unrelated documents.
3. Jump rowSix full bios make a page several screens tall. These pills link to #therapist-N on each band, so a visitor who came for one person reaches them in a click instead of scrolling past five others. Compact band — it is a signpost, not a section.
4. Bio 1 — the founderThe band, not the card, is the container here. A bio runs four or five paragraphs — training, approach, who they work with, what they are like outside the room — and none of that fits in a card body. Shortening it to fit would throw away exactly the material someone uses to choose.

Name is an H2. The credential line under it is a paragraph, not a subheading: it qualifies the name, it does not open a section. Whoever runs the practice opens the page.
5. Bio 2 — flippedPortrait side alternates and every other band is tinted. Not decoration: six identical stacked bands give the eye no boundary between one person and the next, and a reader who looks away loses their place. The flip and the tint make the seam visible without adding a rule or a heading.
6. Bio 3Portrait is a fixed 440px, top-aligned, natural aspect ratio — it does not stretch to the column height the way the Home page's single bio does. Against a bio this long that would render a portrait several times taller than the face needs to be.
7. Bio 4Every band carries an id, so a person can be linked to from anywhere — a service page, an email, a directory listing. The page used to be linkable only from the top.
8. Bio 5Portrait alt text is the person's name, not empty. These images are the content of the page; blank alt hides six people from screen readers and from image search alike.
9. Bio 6 — the internTrainees close the roster. Same treatment as everyone else — the order carries the seniority, the styling does not need to.
